# ShopEase Payment, Billing, and Dispute Resolution Policy

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
ShopEase supports secure payment options across web and mobile platforms:
- **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)**: Instant zero-fee checkout via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and QR code scan.
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Maestro, and American Express with 3D Secure mandatory OTP verification.
- **Net Banking**: Supported across over 50 major commercial and cooperative banks.
- **ShopEase Wallet and Gift Vouchers**: Instant one-click checkout using preloaded balances or e-gift codes.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available on cart totals under ₹10,000 for serviceable postal codes. A handling fee of ₹29 applies to COD orders.

## 2. Failed Payments and Automated Reversals
- **Session Drop or Gateway Timeout**: If an error message displays during payment, or if the browser closes before redirection to the order confirmation page:
  - Check your bank statement and SMS alerts. If funds were debited but no ShopEase Order ID was generated, the transaction is marked as "Incomplete/Dropped" at the banking gateway.
  - The payment gateway (Razorpay/PayU) initiates an automated reversal within **24 hours**.
  - Funds return to your source account automatically within **3 to 5 business days** (for UPI and Net Banking) or **5 to 7 business days** (for credit/debit cards).
  - Customers do not need to raise a dispute if the failure occurred within the last 48 hours, as banking clearing networks resolve this automatically.

## 3. Duplicate Charges and Double Deductions (High Priority)
- **Definition**: A duplicate charge occurs when a customer's payment method is debited multiple times for a single Order ID or a single checkout attempt.
- **Immediate Escalation Rule**: Any report of duplicate deductions or multiple debits is classified as **HIGH PRIORITY / HUMAN ESCALATION**.
- **Customer Action**: Customers should provide:
  1. The ShopEase Order ID (if generated).
  2. The exact amount and timestamp of each deduction.
  3. The Bank Reference Number (UTR / RRN / Transaction ID) from their SMS alert or statement.
- **Billing Specialist Resolution SLA**: Our dedicated Billing & Finance team verifies bank settlement logs within **12 to 24 hours** and issues a direct refund for the redundant deduction. Funds reflect within 2 to 4 business days.

## 4. Unauthorized Transactions and Card Fraud (Urgent Escalation)
- **Zero-Tolerance Fraud Protection**: If a customer suspects that their card, UPI VPA, or ShopEase account was used without consent:
  - The inquiry triggers **URGENT HUMAN ESCALATION** immediately.
  - The customer support agent places an instant freeze on associated orders to intercept warehouse fulfillment and courier dispatch.
  - The account is temporarily locked to prevent unauthorized modifications.
  - Our Fraud Prevention and Risk Mitigation Unit reviews the IP address, device fingerprint, and shipping address, and assists the customer with banking chargeback documentation.

## 5. Invoices, GST Numbers, and Billing Errors
- Digital tax invoices containing official GSTIN numbers are sent automatically to the customer's registered email upon order dispatch.
- Business buyers can add their corporate GST identification number during the checkout step to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC). Note that B2B GST details cannot be retroactively modified once an invoice has been generated.
- In the event of an overcharge, incorrect tax calculation, or missing promotional discount, billing adjustments are verified and credited as store credit or source refund within 24 hours.
