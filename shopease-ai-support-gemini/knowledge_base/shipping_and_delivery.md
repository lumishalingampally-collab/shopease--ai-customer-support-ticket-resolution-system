# ShopEase Shipping, Tracking, and Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Tiers and Service Timelines
ShopEase delivers across over 19,000 postal codes nationwide. Customers can select from three delivery tiers:
- **Standard Shipping**:
  - Delivery window: **3 to 5 business days** for Tier-1 and Tier-2 metropolitan cities; **5 to 7 business days** for regional and rural areas.
  - Cost: Free on all orders with cart value of **₹499 or above**. A standard flat shipping charge of ₹49 applies to orders below ₹499.
- **Express Shipping**:
  - Delivery window: **1 to 2 business days** (available in select metropolitan cities).
  - Cost: Additional ₹99 express surcharge. Free for ShopEase Plus Gold members.
- **Same-Day Priority Delivery**:
  - Available in select metropolitan postal codes (e.g., Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad).
  - Order cutoff: Must be placed and confirmed before **11:00 AM IST** on business days.
  - Cost: Flat ₹149 delivery surcharge.

## 2. Order Tracking and Status Definitions
- Once an order leaves our fulfillment center, an SMS and email notification containing the courier partner name (e.g., BlueDart, Delhivery, Ecom Express) and Airway Bill (AWB) tracking number are sent.
- Customers can track live status directly under "My Orders" or by entering the AWB number on the carrier's tracking portal.
- **Common Tracking Statuses**:
  - *Order Placed*: Payment confirmed, warehouse inventory reserved.
  - *Processing*: Item picked, packaged, and labeled in the fulfillment center.
  - *Dispatched / In Transit*: Package handed over to logistics carrier and moving through distribution hubs.
  - *Out for Delivery*: Courier delivery executive is in your locality for doorstep delivery.
  - *Failed Delivery Attempt*: Courier attempted delivery but recipient was unavailable or address unreachable. Carriers will re-attempt delivery on the next business day (maximum 3 attempts before returning to origin).

## 3. Delayed Deliveries and Escalation Protocol
- **Allowable Weather/Logistics Window**: Regional festivals, severe weather conditions, highway blockades, or local holidays may delay transit by up to **48 to 72 hours**.
- **Delivery Delay Threshold**:
  - If a package is delayed beyond **3 business days** past the estimated delivery date, the ticket must be flagged for Logistics Investigation.
  - If the tracking status shows zero hub movement or scan updates for **4 consecutive business days**, the package is categorized as **Stalled / Potential Lost in Transit** and must be escalated to a human specialist.
- **Late Delivery Compensation**: For orders with paid Express or Same-Day shipping that miss their guaranteed window due to ShopEase logistics failure, the premium shipping surcharge is refunded automatically, along with a ₹100 apology wallet credit.

## 4. Package Marked Delivered but Not Received
If your tracking status updates to "Delivered" but you have not received your parcel:
1. Verify with household members, building security, neighbors, or society reception desks.
2. In rare cases, delivery executives prematurely mark parcels as delivered while concluding their delivery route. Please wait up to **4 hours**.
3. If the parcel is still missing after 4 hours, file a "Non-Receipt of Delivered Package" ticket within **48 hours** of the delivery timestamp.
4. Our logistics investigations team contacts the delivery executive for GPS geo-stamp coordinates and digital proof of delivery (POD signature/photo). If courier misdelivery is established, an immediate priority replacement or full refund is issued.
