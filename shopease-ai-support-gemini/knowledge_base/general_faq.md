# ShopEase General Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Customer Support Operating Hours and Languages
- **Operating Hours**: The automated AI Assistant operates 24/7. Human support specialists are available daily from **8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST**, 7 days a week.
- **Multilingual Support**: Support is offered in **English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Telugu (తెలుగు)**. Inquiries submitted in other regional languages will receive an automated translated response with human specialist coordination.
- **Support Channels**: Live web/app chat, email support (`support@shopease.example.com`), and priority telephone callback for escalated tickets.

## 2. Product Warranty and Repair Support
- All electronic goods and branded appliances purchased through ShopEase carry official manufacturer brand warranty.
- To claim a manufacturer warranty:
  - Download your GST invoice from the "My Orders" tab on ShopEase.
  - Present the invoice and product serial number to an authorized brand service center.
  - ShopEase does not directly repair items outside the 14-day return window, but our support team will provide service center contact information and escalate unresolved brand disputes.

## 3. Gift Cards and Wallet Balances
- ShopEase digital gift cards have a validity period of **1 year (365 days)** from the date of purchase.
- Gift card balances cannot be withdrawn to external bank accounts, converted to cash, or transferred to third-party wallets.
- In the event of an order cancellation or return paid via Gift Card, the refund is restored back to the active gift card balance.

## 4. International Shipping Restrictions
- ShopEase currently fulfills and delivers orders **exclusively within India** across serviceable pin codes.
- International shipping, cross-border freight forwarding, and international card billing without 3D Secure verification are not supported.

## 5. Offline Stores and Click-and-Collect
- ShopEase is currently a 100% digital e-commerce platform. We do not operate offline brick-and-mortar retail stores or click-and-collect pickup counters. All deliveries are made directly to the customer's specified doorstep shipping address.
