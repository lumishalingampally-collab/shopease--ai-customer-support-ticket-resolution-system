# ShopEase Promotions, Discount Coupons, and Cashback Policy

## 1. Applying Discount Promo Codes
- Promotional voucher codes must be entered in the "Apply Coupon" field on the Cart or Checkout screen prior to payment authorization.
- Coupon discounts cannot be applied retroactively to orders that have already been finalized and paid.
- **Minimum Purchase Thresholds**: Most coupon codes specify minimum pre-tax cart values (e.g., code `SAVE20` requires a minimum cart of ₹999).
- **Single Coupon Rule**: Only **one** promotional coupon code can be redeemed per transaction. Stacking multiple discount coupons is not supported.

## 2. Bank and Payment Partner Offers
- Instant discount promotions affiliated with partner banks (e.g., HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis) require payment using the designated bank's eligible credit or debit card during checkout.
- Instant discounts are subtracted directly from the payable amount on the checkout summary screen.
- Payment gateway cashback campaigns (e.g., Paytm or CRED cashback) are credited directly by the payment partner to the customer's wallet or card account within **7 to 14 business days**.

## 3. Coupons on Cancelled or Returned Orders
- **Cancelled Orders**:
  - If a customer cancels an entire order that utilized a single-use coupon, the coupon code is reinstated and becomes active in their account within **2 hours**.
- **Partially Returned Orders**:
  - If a customer returns a portion of a multi-item order that qualified for a tiered coupon discount, the coupon discount is prorated across all items.
  - If the residual retained items drop the order below the coupon's minimum spending threshold, the discount value is recouped from the net refund balance.

## 4. Expiry and Non-Transferability
- Promotional codes feature strict expiration timestamps based on Indian Standard Time (IST).
- Promotional discount codes cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred between accounts, or combined with employee discount quotas.
