# ShopEase Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Product Policy

## 1. Reporting Window and Photographic Evidence
- **Reporting Window**: Customers must report items received in damaged condition, defective state, or with incorrect items dispatched within **48 hours** of package delivery.
- **Evidence Requirement**:
  - Clear photographs showing the shipping label on the outer box.
  - Clear photographs or a short video demonstrating the product defect, damage, or the incorrect item received.
  - For electronic devices showing no power or screen malfunction, a brief unboxing or power-on video is recommended.

## 2. Wrong Product Dispatched by ShopEase
- If you receive an entirely different product or model from what was ordered:
  - Do not discard the packaging or barcode labels.
  - Customer Support will arrange an **Express Doorstep Swap** within 24 to 48 hours.
  - The delivery agent collects the incorrect merchandise and hands over the verified correct product in a single visit wherever inventory permits.
  - If the correct product is out of stock, an immediate full refund plus a ₹150 apology voucher is processed upon pickup.

## 3. Damaged or Defective Goods Resolution
- **Immediate Replacement Priority**: A free replacement unit is dispatched within 24 hours of return pickup verification.
- **Manufacturer Technical Inspection**: For premium electronics and appliances valued above ₹15,000, a manufacturer-authorized brand technician visits the customer's address within 48 hours to inspect physical vs. manufacturing defects and issue a Technical Defect Certificate (TDC).
- **Full Refund Alternative**: If the customer prefers a refund rather than a replacement for damaged or defective merchandise, a 100% refund (including original shipping fees) is issued to the payment source upon warehouse receipt.
