# ShopEase Account Security and Profile Management Policy

## 1. Password Reset and Login Assistance
- **Self-Service Password Reset**: Customers can initiate a password reset via the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in screen by providing their registered email address or mobile number.
- **OTP Validation**: A 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) or encrypted reset link is dispatched.
  - The OTP/reset link is valid for exactly **15 minutes** and expires after 3 unsuccessful entry attempts.
  - If you do not receive the OTP within 2 minutes, check your email spam folder or request a voice OTP call.
- **Account Lockout Safeguard**: Entering an incorrect password 5 consecutive times temporarily locks account access for **30 minutes** as a brute-force prevention measure.

## 2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Customers can enable Two-Factor Authentication under "Account Settings" > "Security & Privacy".
- Supported 2FA channels: SMS One-Time Passcodes and Time-Based Authenticator Apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy).
- **Mandatory 2FA**: 2FA verification is strictly mandatory for:
  - Orders with checkout totals exceeding **₹50,000**.
  - Changing registered bank account details for COD refund deposits.
  - Updating registered email addresses or primary mobile contact numbers.

## 3. Modifying Profile Contact Information
- **Updating Registered Mobile or Email**:
  - Requires simultaneous dual-channel verification (OTP sent to the old contact method + OTP sent to the newly requested contact method).
  - If a customer has lost access to their old phone number or email, they must contact Support for manual identity verification (submitting government photo ID and order verification).

## 4. Suspicious Account Activity and Security Lockdown
- If you observe orders you did not place, unauthorized address additions, or suspicious login location notifications:
  - Immediately notify ShopEase Support to place an **Emergency Account Freeze**.
  - The security operations team will terminate all active browser/mobile sessions across all devices.
  - Ongoing fulfillment on unauthorized orders is halted immediately.
  - Representatives will **NEVER** ask for your password, credit card CVV, or banking UPI PIN.
