# ShopEase Returns, Refunds, and Exchanges Policy

## 1. Return Window and Eligibility Overview
- **Standard Return Window**: Customers can initiate a return within **30 calendar days** from the verified delivery date for most categories, including clothing, footwear, home essentials, and lifestyle accessories.
- **Electronics & Gadgets**: Smartphones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, and audio equipment have a restricted **14-calendar-day** return window due to serial number registration and data security policies.
- **Perishable & Personal Care Exceptions**: Cosmetics, innerwear, swimwear, groceries, perishable goods, and customized/monogrammed products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged, defective, or past their expiration date.
- **Clearance Items**: Products explicitly tagged as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" are non-returnable and non-refundable, except in cases of manufacturing defects verified by our technical team.

## 2. Product Condition Requirements for Returns
To qualify for an approved return and full refund:
- Items must remain in brand-new, unworn, unwashed, and undamaged condition.
- All original manufacturer tags, security labels, dust bags, and warranty cards must remain firmly attached and untampered with.
- The item must be repacked in its original brand box and outer packaging.
- Free gifts, bundled items, or promotional accessories that accompanied the purchase must be returned alongside the primary product. If gifts are omitted, their retail value will be deducted from the net refund amount.

## 3. Product Exchanges
- **Eligible Exchanges**: Exchanges are offered exclusively for alternate sizes and colors of the exact same product model, subject to real-time inventory availability.
- **Exchange Window**: Must be submitted within 30 days of delivery (14 days for electronics).
- **Price Differences**: If an exchange is requested for a variant that currently has a different listed price, the original purchase price is honored without additional charges or credits.
- **Exchange Timeline**: Once the returned item is collected by our courier partner, the replacement unit is dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.

## 4. Return Request and Pickup Procedure
1. Navigate to "My Orders" on the ShopEase website or mobile app and tap "Request Return / Exchange".
2. Select the specific item, choose the applicable reason code, and upload photos if the reason involves damage or discrepancies.
3. A prepaid shipping label and digital QR code will be generated immediately.
4. Our doorstep courier partner will arrive for pickup within **2 to 3 business days**.
5. The courier representative conducts a preliminary doorstep verification (checking item condition, tags, and product serial number).

## 5. Inspection and Refund Timelines
After the returned item arrives at the ShopEase central fulfillment center, quality inspection is finalized within 48 hours:
- **ShopEase Wallet / Store Credit**: Credited instantly (0 to 2 hours) upon inspection approval. Wallet credits never expire and apply to all catalog items.
- **UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)**: Recredited within **24 to 48 business hours** to the original linked bank account.
- **Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex)**: Processed within **5 to 7 business days**, subject to your issuing bank's settlement cycle.
- **Net Banking**: Reflected within **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Customer must provide bank account details (Account Number, IFSC, Account Holder Name) in the app; NEFT/IMPS transfer is executed within **3 business days** of bank detail submission.

## 6. Return Shipping Charges
- Returns initiated due to defective items, transit damage, or incorrect items dispatched by ShopEase are completely **free of charge**.
- Discretionary returns (such as "buyer changed mind" or "found cheaper elsewhere") enjoy free returns for ShopEase Plus members, while standard accounts incur a nominal reverse logistics fee of ₹49, deducted from the final refund.
