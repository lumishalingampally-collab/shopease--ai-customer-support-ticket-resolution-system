# ShopEase Order Cancellation and Modification Policy

## 1. Order Cancellation Window and Eligibility
- **Self-Service Instant Cancellation Window**: Customers can cancel an order with a single click directly from the "My Orders" dashboard within **2 hours** of placing the order, provided the order remains in "Processing" status.
- **Cancellation Prior to Dispatch**: If the 2-hour window has elapsed but the order has NOT yet transitioned to "Dispatched / In Transit", customer support can submit an internal cancellation request to the fulfillment warehouse.
- **Ineligible for Cancellation (Post-Dispatch)**: Once an order has been assigned an AWB tracking number and marked "Dispatched", warehouse cancellation is locked out. Customers should refuse the delivery when the courier arrives at their doorstep ("RTO - Return to Origin").

## 2. Cancellation Refund Processing
- **Prepaid Orders (UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards)**: Cancellation triggers an automated refund initiation within **2 hours** of cancellation confirmation. Funds credit to the original payment source in **2 to 5 business days**.
- **ShopEase Wallet Balance**: Refunded instantly (0 to 15 minutes) into the customer's wallet balance.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders**: No financial transaction has taken place; the order record is marked as void without monetary charge.

## 3. Order Modifications and Address Changes
- **Modifying Shipping Address**:
  - Shipping addresses can be amended free of charge if the order is still in "Order Placed" or early "Processing" status.
  - Navigate to "Order Details" > "Edit Shipping Address" or contact Customer Support.
  - **Limitation**: State/Province changes are not permitted due to interstate GST tax regulations; only local street address, apartment number, and recipient phone number updates within the same postal jurisdiction are supported.
  - Once the order is dispatched, carrier address rerouting is not guaranteed and requires human support coordination with the courier dispatch team.
- **Item Swapping, Size Changes, and Cart Quantity Updates**:
  - ShopEase's automated inventory reservation system does not support swapping items, changing item colors/sizes, or altering item quantities in an active order.
  - To change items, customers must cancel the current order within the allowable window and place a new order with their preferred product selections.
